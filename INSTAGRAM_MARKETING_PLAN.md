## 🎬 Reel Ideas (15-60 seconds)

## 🎨 Brand Guidelines for Posts

### Colors
- **Primary**: Purple (#8b5cf6) to Blue (#3b82f6) gradient
- **Accent**: Yellow (#f59e0b) for highlights
- **Text**: White on dark, Dark gray on light
- **Background**: Clean white or dark navy

### Fonts
- **Headlines**: Bold, modern sans-serif (Poppins, Inter)
- **Body**: Clean, readable (Roboto, Open Sans)
- **Quotes**: Elegant serif (Playfair, Merriweather)

### Visual Style
- **Screenshots**: Always use both light and dark mode
- **Icons**: Consistent emoji usage
- **Spacing**: Clean, minimal, breathing room
- **Overlays**: Semi-transparent for readability

### Logo Placement
- Bottom right corner for posts
- Top left for reels
- Watermark: Semi-transparent

## 📝 Caption Best Practices

### Hook (First Line)
- Use emoji + strong statement
- Ask a question
- Make a bold claim
- Create curiosity

### Body
- Keep it concise (150-300 characters ideal)
- Use line breaks for readability
- Include 2-3 key points
- Add social proof when possible

### Call-to-Action
- "Try it now! Link in bio"
- "Save this for later 📌"
- "Tag someone who needs this"
- "What's your habit goal? 👇"

### Hashtags
**Primary** (Always use):
#HabitFlow #HabitTracker #BuildGoodHabits

**Category-Specific**:
- Productivity: #ProductivityApp #GetThingsDone #TimeManagement
- Motivation: #MotivationMonday #InspireDaily #PersonalGrowth
- Wellness: #HealthyHabits #WellnessJourney #SelfCare
- Tech: #ProductivityTools #AppReview #TechForGood

**Trending** (Research weekly):
- Check Instagram's trending hashtags
- Use 3-5 trending tags per post
- Mix popular (1M+) and niche (10k-100k)

## 🎯 Engagement Strategy

### Post Engagement
1. **Reply to all comments within 1 hour**
2. **Ask questions in captions** to encourage comments
3. **Use polls in stories** for quick engagement
4. **Repost user content** (with permission) to Stories

### Growth Tactics
1. **Collaborate with micro-influencers** (10k-100k followers)
2. **Run contests**: "Best streak wins a feature spotlight"
3. **Create shareable content**: Infographics, quote posts
4. **Cross-promote**: Share IG posts to Twitter, LinkedIn

### Story Strategy (Daily)
- Behind-the-scenes development
- Quick tips (15s videos)
- Polls: "What feature should we add?"
- User shoutouts
- "Link in bio" reminders

## 📊 Analytics & Optimization

### Track These Metrics
- **Engagement Rate**: Likes + Comments / Followers
- **Reach**: How many unique accounts saw your post
- **Saves**: High-value content indicator
- **Profile Visits**: Interest in learning more
- **Link Clicks**: Actual conversions to app

### Optimize Based On
- **Best Posting Times**: Adjust based on your analytics
- **Top Performing Content**: Double down on what works
- **Worst Performers**: Learn what to avoid
- **Story Completion Rate**: Keep stories engaging

### Monthly Review
- Which content pillar performs best?
- Which hashtags drive most reach?
- What time slots get best engagement?
- Adjust strategy for next month
